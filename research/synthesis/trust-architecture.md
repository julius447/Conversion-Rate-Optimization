# Trust & Proof System Architecture — the ONE system every template plugs into

**Lens:** Trust & proof system architect (adversarial integration layer)
**Inputs:** all 14 template deep-dives + all 20 block audits + context/data files (read in full). Every claim quoted below was live-verified by a phase-1/2 agent on 2026-08-02; nothing here is invented. Where agents disagree, the disagreement is named and resolved with the trade-off stated.

**Doctrine applied by name:** Candour gate (rating + count + source or removed; no invented proof), Cialdini authority & social proof (proof persuades only when verifiable and similar), MECLABS heuristic C = 4m + 3v + 2(i−f) − 2a (anxiety relief must sit AT the ask, not below it), Baymard (specificity beats vagueness; labeled trust seals beat anonymous logos), NN/g (banner blindness for unlabeled logo walls; surface errors read as workmanship errors), Google message match (extended to internal promise consistency), Jakob's law (one thing = one name), JTBD (the Swedish homeowner's verification ritual: Elsäkerhetsverket registry check + Konsumentverket written-quote norm).

**The core diagnosis this file resolves:** the site does not lack proof — it has the *best* proof in the category (a government-registry deep link pre-queried with Ampy's record, 12 real named/dated Google reviews, 6 deep E-E-A-T electrician profiles, an anchored 25-review badge on articles). But the proof system is (a) internally contradictory (three different installation counts, four different response promises, two prices for the same product), (b) unanchored precisely where the ask happens (bare "5.0" ×4–6 per page), (c) sequenced BELOW the ask (Certificates 6–12 blocks under MainContact on 289/290 pages; TeamSection after the form on all 85 carriers), and (d) disconnected (6 orphan expert pages; About page with zero faces). The Clarity 47s paid visitor who went Kontakt → Om oss and died there is the behavioral proof of unmet trust demand. The fix is ONE canonical claims-set + ONE proof ladder + ONE link mesh + ONE SLA hierarchy, consumed by every template.

## 2. THE PROOF LADDER (per template position)

One system, five rungs. Each rung has ONE job and ONE format; a claim lives on exactly one rung per page (plus the capped rating badge). This resolves the cross-audit tension between "put proof adjacent to the ask" (Certificates audit, score 1740) and "don't repeat identical proof into banner blindness" (CTA-bands audit): the SAME facts appear at most twice, in DIFFERENT formats (strip vs one-liner), each mounted where a specific anxiety fires.

### 2.1 Rung 1 — Hero trust-row (all hero variants: Hero_2, Hero-1, AlternativHero-v2)
- Content: canonical anchored badge **"5,0 av 5 · 25 omdömen på Google"** (linked GBP, target=_blank rel=noopener). Homepage additionally keeps its volume line ("Över 3 000 installationer per år" — post [GAP-2]).
- Job: 5-second legitimacy. Nothing else — no logo walls in heroes.
- AlternativHero gets the trust/meta ACF slot (off by default; on for hubs + lead magnets) per AH-06 — team/news variants stay untouched (approved-rendering rule).
- Mobile: the row must not be occluded by the cookie banner at first paint (live-verified defect on homepage — coordinate with consent-banner placement).

### 2.2 Rung 2 — Post-hero AUTHORITY STRIP (the Elsäkerhetsverket verification strip — full spec)
**This is the one new component in the whole trust system.** It replaces the banned-claim Metrics block on the 114 geo/pillar pages and mounts post-hero on service (22), elektriker-för-X (13, B2B variant), product (26, compressed into hero column) and lead-magnet wraps.

Spec:
- **Format:** one-row light band (NOT another navy card — it must separate from the dark hero; the dark-stacking finding), 3 labeled claim-cells + optional 4th per intent. Height ≤ 96px desktop, 2-col wrap ≤780px, min tap targets 44px. NO `hidden-on-load`, NO enterView fade — proof never depends on JS to exist (CERT-05/MET-08).
- **Cell 1 (lead, visually dominant):** Elsäkerhetsverket mark + claim heading, H2-level text: "**Registrerat elinstallationsföretag — kontrollera oss själva i Elsäkerhetsverkets register**" → deep link `https://www.elsakerhetsverket.se/kollaelforetaget/foretagsregister/?foretag=12047521` (target=_blank, rel=noopener, alt="Elsäkerhetsverkets företagsregister — Ampy Nordic AB"). Rationale: the exact check Swedish authorities tell consumers to run; inviting self-verification is a costly, falsifiable signal — candour-native proof (Certificates audit).
