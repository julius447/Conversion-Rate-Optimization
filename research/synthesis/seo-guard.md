## 3. Internal-linking doctrine post-restructure

The restructure kills blocks, merges tails, retargets ~900 CTAs and rewires the geo mesh. This doctrine is the target state; the **conservation rule** governs every individual change:

> **Link-conservation rule:** before any block is killed, merged or retargeted, inventory its internal links (in-text ACF anchors included) and re-home every link that points at a rank-relevant URL. Links to /kontakt/ are exempt (utility page; header/footer coverage suffices). No page may drop below 2 crawlable inlinks (nav/sitemap excluded).

### 3.1 Geo corpus: rotating mesh → hub-and-spoke (the doorway fix)

1. **Build the "Alla områden" hub** — one indexable page (or one per CPT, tabbed) listing all 56 orter × 4 CPTs with stable, crawlable links and one line of real differentiation per ort where available. This becomes the ONE permanent inlink source every geo page can rely on. Link it from: the MapBlock sub-card (replacing the "Osäker ifall…?" /kontakt/ ask), the prefooter Områden column, and each pillar.
2. **MapBlock becomes deterministic:** nearest-10 by geography (stable across cache regens, proximity-sorted, self-excluded) + the "Alla områden" hub link. Never random, never 20. Rationale: accumulating signals beat churning ones; nearest-N is also the honest UX ("eljour nära dig"). This cuts the geo cross-mesh from ~5 300 rotating links to ~2 650 stable ones plus a hub spine — hub-and-spoke replaces the interlinked-doorway lattice.
3. **Pillars own their children:** each pillar (elektriker/eljour/elinstallation/laddbox) gets a full A–Z ort index (behind the MapBlock or as a linked section) so all 56 children are 1 click from their pillar. Children already link up via breadcrumbs; keep them.
4. **Differentiate, don't multiply:** the per-intent content forks (R17) are part of the doorway defense — every geo page must keep its unique ContentBlock/FAQ/VissteDuAtt text through all re-sequencing (R5/R6 conditions).
5. **Prefooter Områden:** 6–10 curated top-demand orter (solna, norrtälje, vallentuna per GSC demand) + "Alla områden →". Not 56.

### 3.2 Hubs and services

6. /elservice/ grid: real links, grouped Akut/Eljour-first; the 4 orphan services get grid links AND at least one nav/prefooter mention. /laddboxar/ + /solcellsbatterier/: keep full assortment in served DOM; product pages keep "Liknande" capped at 4 **with** a hub link.
7. Mega-menu: column headings link their pillars; Eljour to top level; every nav label = destination H1.

### 3.3 Articles ↔ services ↔ calculators (the assist loop)

8. Every article keeps its in-body service/geo links (already good in shell posts) and gains the "Nästa steg" card: service page (prefilled form param) + matching calculator. Category map: elcentral cluster → /elservice/elcentral/ + Elcentral-kollen; laddbox articles → /laddbox/ + Laddboxkalkylatorn; rot/grön-teknik → Energikalkylatorn.
9. Matching service pages gain a 3-card topical ArticleCards instance (ART-06) — the missing forward edge of the loop.
10. Calculators exit orphanhood: nav (corrected labels), pillar embeds (/laddbox/ gets Laddboxkalkylatorn per PIL-13), article Nästa steg cards, and the magnet wrap's MainContact links back to services.
