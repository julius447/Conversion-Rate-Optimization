## Why it beats status quo
MECLABS HealthSpire: longer pages win when the added content answers real decision questions — this IS the decision content; NN/g: critical purchase information must not require discovery. Surfacing scope turns the price from a contested claim ("Totalt" vs FAQ range) into a defined package ("Paketpris standardinstallation efter Grön Teknik: X kr — här är exakt vad det täcker"), which is the only honest way to keep the strike-through-free price anchor the product verdict recommends. It also pre-qualifies leads (visitors with gräv/uppsäkring needs self-identify before the call — saves qualification time against the ~2 660 kr täckningsbidrag economics).

**Adversarial note — the scare-off objection:** listing exclusions may depress raw form submits. Named trade-off: it raises *qualified* submits and protects close rate; the popup already discloses these terms to anyone who digs, so the only thing hiding them buys today is a worse first phone call. Guardrail metric in the PP-06/PP-01 test: scroll-depth past the price block + lead-quality from CRM callback outcomes.
